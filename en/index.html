FAQ

Common questions

I deleted photos, but my free space didn't go up.

iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To free the space right away, empty it in Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted.

Can Airy delete my photos on its own?

No. Every deletion goes through the Review screen, a 3-second Undo and your approval in the iOS confirmation dialog. Widgets and Shortcuts only open a screen; they never delete anything.

Are my photos sent to a server?

No. Airy has no servers. All comparison and analysis happens on your iPhone.

I use iCloud Photos.

If iCloud Photos is on, photos you delete on your iPhone are also deleted on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Airy reminds you of this the first time you clean up.

How is the free allowance counted?

Up to 2 GB on the first day you clean, then up to 100 MB every day after that. It resets at midnight.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Airy. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.
